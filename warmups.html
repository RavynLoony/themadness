À La Carte · Available During September Warm-Ups

Get Out of the House

The outside-event Warm-Ups below can be completed throughout the September Warm-Up weeks, with those points banked until the end of THE MADNESS. The Satanic Mechanic Challenge has its own longer challenge window.

2

Outside Event

Attend an outside Halloween or seasonal event, such as an amusement-park Halloween event or fall festival. House parties do not count.

2

Dark Ride / Walkthrough

Complete a dark ride, corn maze, seasonal walkthrough or haunt, escape room, or fun house.

5

Themed Dark Ride

If the ride, maze, walkthrough, escape room or fun house is specifically asylum, hospital, psychiatric or medical themed, it is worth 5 points instead of 2.

10

Actual Hospital / Asylum Venue

If the event or haunt takes place inside a real current or former hospital, psychiatric / mental hospital, asylum, sanitarium / sanatorium or similar institution, it is worth 10 points.

Multiple walkthroughs: At an attraction with multiple separately named walkthroughs, mazes or houses, each one you complete counts separately. Standard dark-ride / walkthrough points may be claimed separately from the ordinary 2-point Outside Halloween / Seasonal Event value when both apply.

Tim Curry · 2026 R.I.P. ATB

The Satanic Mechanic Challenge · 10 / 20 / 30 Points

Tim Curry will be one of our 2026 R.I.P. ATBs.

This challenge is OPEN DURING WARM-UPS and remains open through the end of THE MADNESS at sunrise Sunday, November 1, 2026, in each Maniac's local time zone.

Attend The Rocky Horror Picture Show (1975) in a movie theater for 10 points, attend a movie-theater screening with a LIVE SHADOW CAST for 20 points total, or attend a live stage production of The Rocky Horror Show for 30 points.

How to claim it

If you complete an outside-event activity or The Satanic Mechanic Challenge during WARM-UPS, make a post in the Madness Facebook group with a photo of the event, yourself there, or your ticket, receipt or admission confirmation. For The Satanic Mechanic Challenge, the challenge points are added to your banked Warm-Up points.

Once the MAIN GAME begins September 27, include the qualifying outing or Satanic Mechanic Challenge in that week's WEEKLY SUMMARY so it is scored in that week's total. We would still love you to share your photos in the group.

Pennhurst Asylum Challenge: Visit Pennhurst Asylum for 25 points. Mileage bonus: Players who live more than 50 miles from Pennhurst can add 1 point per hour of travel. During Warm-Ups, use the same Facebook-post proof process above. Pennhurst Asylum tickets & info →
