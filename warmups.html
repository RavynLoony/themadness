Complete a dark ride, corn maze, seasonal walkthrough or haunt, escape room, or fun house.

Themed Dark Ride

If the ride, maze, walkthrough, escape room or fun house is specifically asylum, hospital, psychiatric or medical themed, it is worth 5 points instead of 2.

Multiple walkthroughs: At an attraction with multiple separately named walkthroughs, mazes or houses, each one you complete counts separately. If the same outing also qualifies as an Outside Halloween / Seasonal Event, claim those 2 event points separately.

How to claim an outside-event Warm-Up

Make a post in the Madness Facebook group with a photo showing the event. It can be a photo of the event itself, you at the event, or your ticket, receipt or admission confirmation if you do not want to be on camera. The Enchantress of Nevermore will award your points on that Facebook post and update the scoreboard.
