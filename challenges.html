Active During September Warm-Ups

À La Carte Outside-Event Warm-Ups

Outside Halloween or seasonal events are worth 2 points. Dark rides, corn mazes, seasonal walkthroughs or haunts, escape rooms and fun houses are worth 2 points each, or 5 points when specifically asylum, hospital, psychiatric or medical themed. If the event or haunt actually takes place inside a real current or former hospital, psychiatric hospital, mental hospital, asylum, sanitarium / sanatorium or similar institution, it is worth 10 points.

How to claim it

If you complete an outside-event activity during WARM-UPS, make a post in the Madness Facebook group with a photo of the event, yourself there, or your ticket, receipt or admission confirmation. Those Warm-Up points are banked until the end of THE MADNESS. Once the MAIN GAME begins September 27, include the qualifying outing in that week's WEEKLY SUMMARY so it is scored.
