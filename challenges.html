À La Carte Outside-Event Warm-Ups

Outside Halloween or seasonal events are worth 2 points. Dark rides, corn mazes, seasonal walkthroughs or haunts, escape rooms and fun houses are worth 2 points each, or 5 points when specifically asylum, hospital, psychiatric or medical themed. If the event or haunt actually takes place inside a real current or former hospital, psychiatric hospital, mental hospital, asylum, sanitarium / sanatorium or similar institution, it is worth 10 points.

Rocky Horror Live / Theatrical Special

This challenge is OPEN DURING WARM-UPS and remains open through the end of THE MADNESS at sunrise Sunday, November 1, 2026, in each Maniac's local time zone.

Attend a movie-theater screening of The Rocky Horror Picture Show (1975) for 10 points, or attend a live stage production of The Rocky Horror Show for 10 points.

How to claim it

If you complete an outside-event activity or the Rocky Horror special during WARM-UPS, make a post in the Madness Facebook group with a photo of the event, yourself there, or your ticket, receipt or admission confirmation. Those Warm-Up points are banked until the end of THE MADNESS. Once the MAIN GAME begins September 27, you may still post photos or tickets as usual, but you MUST include the qualifying outing or Rocky Horror challenge in that week's WEEKLY SUMMARY so it is scored.
