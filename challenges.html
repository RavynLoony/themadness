À La Carte Outside-Event Warm-Ups

Outside Halloween or seasonal events are worth 2 points. Dark rides, corn mazes, seasonal walkthroughs or haunts, escape rooms and fun houses are worth 2 points each, or 5 points when specifically asylum, hospital, psychiatric or medical themed.

How to claim

Make a post in the Madness Facebook group with a photo showing the event. It can be a photo of the event itself, you at the event, or your ticket, receipt or admission confirmation if you do not want to be on camera. The Enchantress of Nevermore will award the points on that Facebook post and update the scoreboard.
