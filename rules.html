Year 18 · 2026 Game Rules

The Madness Runs the Asylum

Eighteen years ago... Halloween horror watching became a game. The object is still gloriously simple: watch as much accepted horror, Halloween and Madness-adjacent material as you can, keep a weekly record, and talk about what you found.

The 2026 calendar

The Madness begins Sunday, September 27 at 12:01 AM in each Maniac’s own time zone and runs until sunrise Sunday, November 1.

The first weekly summary is due by the end of the day Sunday, October 4, with a one-day grace period. First-week summaries may not be posted before Friday, October 2 at noon EDT.

The final week gets a three-day grace period. Accepted watches may still be added during that grace period. The final-summary deadline is Wednesday, November 4. Once you submit your final summary, your Madness is over.

The Game

How the Madness works

  1. Watch accepted titles. Horror is the center of the game, but the Madness has always had liberal borders. Theme-related and otherwise accepted genres count too.
  2. Post one weekly summary. Summaries should be titled THE MADNESS: RUNS THE ASYLUM WEEK XXXX!
  3. Link the summary to the Madness group and tag the Enchantress of Nevermore and your Team Judge.
  4. You may post early, but once you post, that week is closed for you. Weekly summaries are due by the end of Sunday in your own time zone, except for the stated grace periods.
  5. Keep reviews short. A rating, reaction, a couple of sentences, thumbs, stars, grades, delighted screaming into the institutional intercom, whatever works.
  6. Runtime is mandatory. The scoring system is based on time watched. See the Scoring page for the full math.

Weekly Summaries

What each entry needs

Film or TV series title
Film year
TV season and episode number
Runtime
Rating or short review
Bonus elements and/or R.I.P. ATB claims

Estimated point values are optional. TV episodes from the same series may be grouped together in one entry. Judges handle the final scoring, so explain why you are claiming a bonus or ATB and let them do the arithmetic.

Eligibility

What can you watch?

The borders are intentionally broad. A title can qualify because of its genre, Halloween connection, or a qualifying story element. A witch character makes a title eligible. The Devil appearing as a character makes a title eligible. A movie does not have to spend ninety minutes politely introducing itself as “horror” first.

  • All horror subgenres
  • Horror comedy
  • Ghost stories
  • Ghost-hunting shows
  • Slashers, serial killers & giallo
  • Vampires & werewolves
  • Zombies
  • Witches, folk horror, pagan & Wiccan films
  • Satanic stories
  • Frankenstein, golems & mad science
  • Creature features, kaiju & monsters
  • Mutants & cannibals
  • Outbreak, plague & pestilence
  • Home invasion
  • Psychological & supernatural thrillers
  • Dark fantasy
  • Sci-fi horror & dark science fiction
  • Apocalyptic & dystopian stories
  • Cyberpunk & industrial horror
  • Cult, grindhouse & psychotronic cinema
  • Extreme horror & video nasties
  • Children’s & YA horror
  • Anime horror
  • Foreign horror
  • Film noir when accepted by the Madness
  • Horror and psychotronic anthology television
Theme rule: Out-of-genre titles can still qualify when a character, setting, event or other major element places them inside the Madness rules. This is why something like The Wizard of Oz can enter through the witch-shaped side door.

Nonfiction & Short Form

Docs, reality, mondo, shorts & serials

Documentaries & Reality

Documentaries and reality television may be accepted when they fit the overall Madness theme. Documentaries specifically about the theme are fully accepted.

Mondo

Mondo films may be accepted when they fit the overall theme, but they are not eligible for bonus points.

Under 30 Minutes

Individual horror shorts, trailer reels or serial installments under a half hour do not score à la carte. Watch a collection and the combined running time can count. These collections are not eligible for bonus points.

Scoring

Runtime, bonuses & ATBs

Feature films begin at 61 minutes. Broadcast television is scored by its original slot, so a roughly 22-minute sitcom episode counts as a half-hour slot and a roughly 48-minute drama counts as an hour slot. Longer films round according to the Madness runtime rules.

Accepted titles may claim up to three of the ten 2026 bonus elements. One Automatic Triple Bonus gives the title its maximum score for its runtime. The full point table, bonus elements and 2026 R.I.P. ATBs live on the Scoring page.

Outside the Screening Room

Seasonal events

Accepted outside Halloween and seasonal events are worth 2 points and should be included in the weekly summary. House parties are not included.

  • Seasonal walkthroughs
  • Zombie walks
  • Haunted tours
  • Amusement-park seasonal events
  • Aquarium or zoo seasonal events
  • Fall & harvest festivals with a pumpkin patch
  • Medical & oddity museums
  • Genre-appropriate musicals
  • Genre-appropriate concerts
  • Genre-appropriate exhibits
  • Horror conventions
  • Film festivals
  • Advance or retro theatrical screenings
  • RiffTrax presentations
Dark rides, walkthroughs and other special themed attractions may also earn special scoring when announced for 2026.

Accessibility & Real Life

Personal challenges & work handicaps

Personal Challenges may be approved for outside events. Work Handicaps may be approved for busier Maniacs when they are performance-based or seasonally related. These are approved individually and count toward the overall game as assigned.

The institutional fine print

Do not get hung up on the rules. Learn the bonus elements and R.I.P. ATBs, tell your Judge why something qualifies, and use the rest as the lid of an alarmingly elaborate tabletop game. The point is to watch movies, discover strange shit, and talk about them.
