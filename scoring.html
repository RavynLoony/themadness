Scoring the Madness

The clock matters. The bonus elements matter. And one Automatic Triple Bonus can send the whole score cart rattling down the institutional hallway.

Scoring at a glance

Accepted films and television begin with base points determined by running time. A title may claim up to three of the ten bonus elements below. Hitting a single Automatic Triple Bonus (ATB) gives that title its maximum score for its running time.

0.5

Half-Hour TV

Count the original broadcast slot, including the time commercials would have occupied.

ATB maximum: 3 points.

1

One-Hour TV

A roughly 48-minute episode made for a one-hour broadcast slot counts as one hour.

ATB maximum: 4 points.

2

Feature / Two-Hour Slot

Most feature films begin at 2 base points. Longer films are rounded to the nearest half hour.

A standard two-hour title at ATB: 6 points.

The Ten Bonus Elements

The Madness Runs the Asylum

You only need one qualifying occurrence inside an element to claim that element. A title can score no more than three ordinary bonus elements. ATB callouts below immediately award the title its maximum value.

  1. A hospital, asylum, psychiatric institution, medical institution, clinic or similar facility is shown.
    ATB: The institution is abandoned.
  2. A main protagonist or antagonist is admitted, committed, confined, discharged or escapes from an institution.
    ATB: A patient impersonates a doctor, nurse, orderly or other institutional staff member.
  3. A patient chart, medical file, X-ray, ID bracelet, specimen or real/stock surgical imagery is shown.
  4. A patient is given or forced to take medication, receives an injection, or visibly avoids taking prescribed medication.
  5. Physical restraints are used, or a padded room, isolation room, seclusion room or solitary cell is shown or used.
  6. Hypnosis or group therapy occurs.
    ATB: Electroconvulsive therapy (ECT) is performed.
  7. Surgery or an operation is performed.
    ATB: A lobotomy, including a transorbital/ice-pick lobotomy, is performed.
  8. A human experiment, experimental treatment, unethical medical experiment or delivery of human organs occurs.
    ATB: Nazi medical experimentation is depicted.
  9. A doctor, nurse, orderly or other institutional staff member is attacked with a medical instrument.
  10. A straitjacket is shown or used.
